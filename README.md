# workout_aftermath
Workout Tracker for Self, Friends and Family

# Bold Body Agenda

A simple, private tracker for the daily basics: food, protein, water, steps, cardio, lifting, HIIT, functional training, sleep and a weekly body check-in. Each day becomes a square on a calendar, and the more goals you hit, the darker it gets.

It's one HTML file with no account, no server, no tracking and no ads.

**Open the app:** https://ramaditya524.github.io/workout_aftermath/
---

## Set it up on your phone

Install it to your Home Screen so it opens full-screen like a normal app and works offline.

**iPhone (Safari)**
1. Open the link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮** in the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

Always open it from the Home Screen icon, not a browser tab. On iPhone, Safari deletes a website's saved data if you use Safari for 7 days without visiting that site. Apps added to the Home Screen are exempt from that rule.

## Where your data lives

- Your data is stored **only on your phone**, inside the app's storage. It is never sent to a server, and nobody else can see it, including whoever shared the link with you.
- Each person who opens the link gets their own separate, private log.
- If you delete the app from your Home Screen, clear your browser's website data, or lose your phone, your log is gone unless you have a backup.

## Backups

Entries save automatically the moment you type them. You don't need to save anything to keep the app up to date.

A backup is a safety copy in case your phone's storage gets wiped:

1. Go to **Settings → Backup → Copy backup**.
2. Paste the text into Apple Notes, Google Keep, an email to yourself, or anywhere else you keep things.

**How often:** about once a week. Each backup is a full copy of everything you've logged, so you only need the most recent one. The app reminds you when your last backup is more than 7 days old.

**To restore,** for example on a new phone: open the app, go to **Settings → Backup**, paste the backup text into "Restore from a backup", and tap **Restore** twice to confirm. This replaces whatever is on that phone.

The backup is your full data written as text (JSON). It is different from the **Summary** tab's "Copy summary", which is a short, readable recap for sharing with friends and can't be restored.

## Ask AI

The Ask AI tab does **not** run any AI and does not send your data anywhere by itself.

It builds a ready-made question containing your goals, a summary and your day-by-day log, copies it, and opens Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. You paste it into a new chat yourself, and free accounts work. Your name is left out, and you can tap **See exactly what gets shared** before sending.

Numbers start to mean something after about **3 weeks** of logging, because daily weight swings with water and salt. The tab shows your progress toward 21 days.

## PDF reports

Go to **Summary → Pick dates**, choose a range, then tap **Open PDF report** and **Save as PDF**. On iPhone, use Share → **Save to Files** in the print screen. The PDF is created on your phone.

## Updating the app

When a new version of `index.html` is uploaded to this repository, reopen the app to get it. Your data stays, because it's stored on your phone, not in the file.
